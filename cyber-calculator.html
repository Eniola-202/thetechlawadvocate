About Harrison & Morrison Insurance Brokers. Harrison & Morrison Nigeria Limited (RC-232884), incorporated on 21 October 1993, is a registered insurance brokerage authorised by the National Insurance Commission (NAICOM), serving individuals and businesses as a trusted intermediary between clients and insurers. The firm facilitates access to cyber insurance and related technology covers, including protection against cyberattacks, ransomware, data breaches, business interruption, digital fraud, technology errors and omissions, and professional liability, subject to insurers’ policy terms and availability. It works with technology, cybersecurity and legal professionals, including Tech Law Advocates.

Important. This calculator gives indicative figures from general benchmarks and industry risk classifications that are starting points, not actuarial conclusions. It is not a quotation, an offer of cover or professional advice. Actual terms depend on insurer underwriting, controls, turnover, data volume, claims history and business interruption exposure.
